Engineering Case Study

How We Automated 136 Certificates with Zero Mismatches

When bulk certificates were dumped from design tools without row-to-file mapping, naive automation would have delivered the wrong certificates to 116 people. Here is the reverse-engineering and verification architecture that solved it.

The Failure Mode

Why Naive Batch Dispatch Fails

Tools like Canva, CorelDRAW, and Google Drive do not export certificates matching your spreadsheet order:

  • Drive ID Scrambling: Google Drive splits files asynchronously, meaning page-001.pdf never correlates with Row 1 of your spreadsheet.
  • Catastrophic Mismatch: Sending by row number causes 100% of participants to receive someone else's certificate.
  • CID Font Encryption: Design tools don't write plain ASCII text; they write hex glyph IDs referencing custom subset tables.
The CertiFlow Solution

Direct Font Stream Reverse-Engineering

Our dual-layer extraction engine solves the root cause by reading inside the PDF objects:

  • CMap Table Decoding: We decompress the PDF's internal /ObjStm streams, locate the /ToUnicode mapping dictionary, and translate hex byte pairs back to unicode characters.
  • Font-Size Isolation: Recipient names are isolated by targeting font size 24.0pt under /F109 (Axiforma-Black), filtering out static titles.
  • Fuzzy & Alias Matching: Token-matching and fuzzy similarity resolve typos, middle names, and inverted order automatically.

The 5-Stage Verification Pipeline

01

Downloaded raw PDF assets directly bypassing Google Drive's 50-file download throttle.
